Questions

How much is a yacht rental in Ain Sokhna?

Prices start from EGP 3,500 for the whole yacht for a full day, depending on the day, the season and the yacht size. Fishing trips for up to 10 guests start from EGP 5,500, and the 2-hour sunset trip (summer only) from EGP 2,000. See the full price table on our yachts page, or message us on WhatsApp (+20 102 881 6191).

What is included?

A full day on your own private yacht from 6 AM to 4 PM, sea access permit, captain and crew, an on-board chef who cooks the food you bring, a grill and free life jackets.

How many guests can the yachts take?

We have yachts for 10, 25 and 30 guests.

Where do we board?

At the marina next to the Portrait Sokhna hotel on the Ain Sokhna – Zafarana road, Attaka, Suez. Please bring a valid ID or passport for each guest.

Can I bring my own food?

Yes. Bring your food and our chef will cook it and serve it on the grill.
